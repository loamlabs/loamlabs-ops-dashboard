Alpina Hoss pages helper

Runs in this Chrome while you are signed in to hasgllc.com. Every 2 or 3 days it loads the 8 Alpina product pages (one at a time, 5 to 10 seconds apart) and sends the stock numbers to your NAS. It never logs in for you: if you are signed out, or Hoss shows a check, a 403 or a 429, it stops, tells the NAS (one email to you), and waits until you click Resume.
